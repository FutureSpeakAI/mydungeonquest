# Waypost / checkpoint-law lessons

**The rule set:** replay is truth; a checkpoint is a proven shortcut. Resumed folds must be byte-identical to the full walk or silently refused (full walk stands in).

- **Resume lanes mirror the reader's substrate WHOLE.** Readers wrap engine functions whole (a direct returns its entire object; consumers pick fields off it). The fold lane must carry the same whole, and parity courts must compare whole-for-whole — an eval comparing a whole return against its inner field reads exactly like real drift and burns a full diagnosis cycle.

- **A shared fold walk inherits the UNION of every caller's inputs.** Before the law, the chart never touched tells; after, one walk feeds all lanes — so a rot fixture (null rows, `narration_blocks: 42`) reached fold seats that had been green for months. Migrating rot-safe readers onto a shared walk exposes every lane to every fixture any caller ever feeds it: prove every lane fail-closed BEFORE the swap, with rot courts in the twin.

- **Guards live at the ONE seat direct and fold share.** Row-rot and bogus-list doors go into the per-entry fold bodies (the seats the composed direct and the walk both stand on) — never into the walk wrapper, or direct and fold diverge on rot and byte-parity lies.

- **Field-rot is a second family beyond row-rot.** A row can BE a plain object yet carry a truthy non-list (`narration_blocks: 42`, `standing_shift: 42`); every bare `x || []` iteration over row-sourced lists crashes on it. Door rows once (`rowsOf`), door every list field (`listOf`/`Array.isArray`), and court BOTH families — two organs held the same hole.

- **Runtime-proven seats must never persist.** The checkpoint seat on the campaign object is trusted because its only roads are the hydrate court (proves + stands) and the seal seat (folds computed in hand). Persisting it into snapshots (save/import/fork/restore) reopens the signature-downgrade door: forged folds wearing REAL copied pins pass the sync record court. Strip such seats at the table's ONE write door (Dexie creating+updating hooks), and court the door adversarially. Sync read-time re-checks (stands) are sound only while every seat road proves.

- **Session trust is identity, not shape.** Store-door stripping alone still leaves the live session: an import/restore RETURN handed straight to state carries any field its file claimed, and a presence-based guard ("field exists, skip re-proof") reads that claim as proof. Brand proven objects in a module-scoped WeakSet at their lawful birth roads only (hydrate court, seal seat) and have the READER refuse unbranded seats — parsed bytes can never forge object identity; the brand rides the checkpoint value itself, so campaign spreads keep it while any re-parse or structuredClone honestly loses it. Import/fork functions return post-door rows, never pre-write drafts.

- **Curtain courts must not dress fixtures in the word.** "Output never speaks the word" convicted a fixture literally TITLED with the word (titles render on the cover). Fixture names stay outside the vocabulary the court polices.

- **Two-natured outputs get two laws.** A keepsake that embeds its own verifier-format chain MUST carry machinery rows in the proof block (the chain cannot verify with rows missing) while rendered pages never speak them. Cut along the proof's unique format marker and hold each nature to its own law — one "never contains X" assertion over the whole file is wrong in both directions.

- **Machinery rows move counted surfaces.** Gates that count journal rows, and wiring needles aimed at replaced call-sites, move with the surface — sweep the WHOLE eval tree for old tokens before rerunning a long chain (moved-surface law).
